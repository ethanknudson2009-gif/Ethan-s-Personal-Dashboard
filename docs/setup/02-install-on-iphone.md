# Setup 2 — Put the dashboard on your iPhone as an app

No App Store needed. This takes about one minute, and you only do it once.

## Steps (iPhone 13, iOS 26)
1. Open **Safari** (it has to be Safari for this step) and go to **https://ethan-s-personal-dashboard.vercel.app**.
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up). On iOS 26 it may be behind the **⋯** button next to the address bar.
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Make sure **Open as Web App** is on, keep the name **Dashboard**, and tap **Add**.
6. Find the new icon (the glowing teal diamond) on your Home Screen and open it.
7. **Sign in once more inside the app.** The installed app keeps its own login, separate from Safari. After this it stays signed in.

## What you get
- It opens **full screen** from its own icon, with no Safari address bar, like a normal app.
- The bottom bar works with your thumb: **Today · School · ＋ · Calendar · Classes**.
- New code I push shows up automatically the next time you open it. There's nothing to update.
- **Notifications** (coming in Phase 2) only work in the installed app, not in Safari. That's an Apple rule.

## Tips
- Drag the icon into your **Dock** so it's always one tap away.
- If the app ever looks stuck, close it (swipe up from the bottom and flick it away) and open it again.
- To remove it: press and hold the icon → **Remove App**. Your data is safe, because it lives in the database, not on the phone.
