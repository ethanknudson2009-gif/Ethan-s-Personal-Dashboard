# Talk to your dashboard: every way, from fastest to most powerful

All of these are free and end up in the same dashboard.

## 1. Inside the app: the Talk button (works right away)
- On **Today**, tap **🎤 Talk**, or **press and hold the ＋** in the bottom bar.
- Say it naturally: *"speech outline due thursday"*, *"anatomy test review friday"*, *"call mom tomorrow"*.
- The words appear as you speak, and the class and day light up as chips. Tap **Add**.
- The first time, iPhone asks for microphone permission. Tap **Allow**.
- If iPhone doesn't allow voice inside the home-screen app, the box opens with the keyboard instead: tap the **🎤 on the keyboard** (bottom right) and speak. It types for you, just as well.

## 2. Hands-free with Siri (phone locked or in your pocket)
Build the **Log It** Shortcut once ([guide 3](03-log-it-shortcut.md), about 10 minutes). Then:
- Say **"Hey Siri, Log It"** → speak → you get a notification like *"📥 To review: Speech: Outline · Thu, Nov 19"*.
- Or press the **Lock Screen button**, or **double-tap the back** of your phone (Back Tap).

It lands in your **Inbox** for a one-tap check, because nothing gets saved wrong without you seeing it.

## 3. A real conversation: Claude as your "AI talker"
Connect Claude once ([guide 4](04-claude-connector.md), about 3 minutes). Then open the **Claude app → voice mode** and just talk:
- *"What do I have this week?"*
- *"Add a World History essay due next Monday."*
- *"I finished the anatomy lab."*
- *"Plan my evening around what's due."*

Claude reads and writes your dashboard directly, and it uses your Claude Pro plan (no extra cost).

## Which should I use?
| Situation | Use |
|---|---|
| Phone in hand, dashboard open | **Talk** button / hold ＋ |
| Walking, phone locked, hands busy | **"Hey Siri, Log It"** |
| Want to think something through or plan | **Claude voice mode** with the connector |
